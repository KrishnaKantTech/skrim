Skrim

Automatically hides your bookmarks bar while you share your screen.

Your bookmarks bar is a list of everything you are working on — clients, offers, the salary spreadsheet, the job search. Screen sharing puts it on the call. Skrim takes it down the moment a share starts and puts every bookmark back, in its original position, the moment it ends.
