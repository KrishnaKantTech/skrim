Your bookmarks are safe

Nothing was ever deleted. While your bar was hidden, every bookmark on it was moved into a folder inside Other bookmarks — they are still in your browser right now, whether or not the extension is.

The one-click way

Install the extension again and open it. It reads the receipt left inside that folder and puts every bookmark back in its original position, then deletes the folder and the placeholder links. You do not have to do any of the steps below.

Or put them back by hand

This works with the extension uninstalled, disabled or broken:

  1. Open the bookmark manager: Ctrl + Shift + O (⌘ + ⌥ + B on a Mac).
  2. In the left sidebar, click Other bookmarks. Look for a folder whose name begins “hidden while screen sharing”.
  3. Open it, select everything except the receipt, and drag it onto your bookmarks bar. The order inside the folder is the order it was in on your bar, so dragging the whole selection at once keeps it.
  4. Delete the placeholder links that were put on your bar in their place.
  5. Delete the now-empty folder and the receipt inside it.
